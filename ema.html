The rules

  • The Trend: 200 EMA sloping (moved at least 1 ATR over 50 candles), price on the trend side.
  • The Tap: price must touch the 200 EMA exactly.
  • The Zone: the EMA sits in a supply zone (sells) or demand zone (buys).
  • The Candles: two very convincing candles after the tap; enter at the close of the 2nd.
  • The Target: take profit at the previous swing high/low. Stop goes beyond the 1st confirmation candle.
  • Risk 1% of the account per trade · one trade per pair at a time · closes after 5 days if neither hits.

Backtest (12 months, all 28 pairs): about 44 trades a year, ~+7R. Few trades means results swing a lot, so give it a few months.
