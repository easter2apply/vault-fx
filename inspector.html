How this test works

Inspector downloaded 6 months of 15-minute, 1-hour and 4-hour candles with real bid and ask prices, found every pullback, crossover, 15-minute and London-breakout setup the bot would have seen, and ran the bot's own council code on each one. Each setup was played forward on 15-minute candles: buys exit on the bid, sells on the ask, so the spread is paid. If the stop and target were both touched in the same candle it counts as a loss. Approved trades move their stop to entry at +1R, like the live bot; one trade per pair at a time. Not simulated: the news blackout, the daily loss limit, the losing-streak breaker, the 3-open-trades and same-dollar limits, and learned vote weights (all officers count equally here). Results are in R: +1.5R is a full win, −1R a full loss.
